## Corrections and new checks

- Reconstruction norm ratio is now labeled as a norm ratio. The additional explained-change score is 1 minus unrepresented-change energy divided by total hidden-change energy. It can be negative and is not a bounded variance partition. The saved per-context vectors independently reproduce this score.
- Random directions are normalized and scaled to their paired decoder norms, then use identical context indices, coefficients, panels and clean forward passes. The old unmatched random runs remain labeled exploratory.
- Nuisance regression and predictor scaling are fitted inside each training fold. Constant forecasts, rank-redundant predictors and degenerate bootstrap intervals are reported as undefined instead of scoring numerical noise.
- Both context-pool halves retain the same 300 features. Their correlation estimates reproducibility within one ordered corpus pool, conditional on one dictionary. Independent corpus draws and dictionary reseeds remain untested.
- Small-model coefficient sweeps include 0.5, 1, 2, 4 and a firing-conditional q95 scale. Dense-panel exclusion, KL change, residual-space change, centered PC1 and anti-aligned-context summaries are included. Coefficient robustness remains untested for Gemma and Llama.
- Holm and Benjamini-Hochberg adjustments are reported per setting/target/control family. Controls themselves are excluded as tested predictors; undefined tests retain their place in the remaining family. Ranking does not establish a difference between the first and second predictor.

The Llama paired run was restarted after a stalled GPU batch. The retry adds stage logging and temporary checkpoints, and uses CPU for the paired-control eigensolve. CPU/CUDA PC1 ratios agreed within 1e-6 on five centered test clouds; this check does not identify the original stall's root cause. Each paired output records its eigensolver device.

## Literature implications

The [dated literature update](RECENT_RESEARCH_2026-09-07.md) checks primary sources through papers submitted on 4 September. Ong et al. already forecast behavioral side effects from unsteered representations; Upadhyaya and Sikdar already test low-entanglement feature selection against behavioral collateral. The defensible contribution is this specific four-setting controlled feature analysis and its robustness measurements. None of the checked papers directly refutes these particular estimates. A matched comparison of vanilla and overlap-regularized dictionaries remains future work, alongside existing geometry-guided intervention controls.
