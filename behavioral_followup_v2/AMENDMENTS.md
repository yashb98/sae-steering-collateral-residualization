# Protocol amendments, 19 September 2026

No v2 model generations or activation measurements had been made when this revision was written. The earlier draft is preserved byte for byte in [PROTOCOL_DRAFT_2026-09-19_INITIAL.md](PROTOCOL_DRAFT_2026-09-19_INITIAL.md).

1. **Correct the ceiling explanation.** Perfect unsteered accuracy leaves room to measure deterioration. v1 recorded no correctness changes; that does not identify the cause. The old assertion that supplying answers made degradation impossible is unsupported.
2. **Disclose adaptation.** The new experiment is explicitly motivated by observed v1 outcomes. The old statement that no v1 held-out outcome informed v2 rules was inaccurate. Fresh evaluation data, not denial of this adaptation, provides prospective separation.
3. **Remove unsupported power claims.** The draft's claims of roughly 5-point detection with four pairs and half that with ten pairs were not backed by a simulation or variance model. They must not be cited. Pilot-based design simulations are pending.
4. **Do not force an unvalidated damage regime.** The old fallback to coefficient 8 after failure of the sensitivity gate would not establish a useful behavioral test. Validate damage sensitivity and output quality before a full selector comparison.
5. **Require intended benefit.** Noninferiority between low/high groups is insufficient when neither improves on unsteered generation. The new saved-output audit motivates a prospective baseline-benefit check.
6. **Separate the links being tested.** Add measurements of actual activation collateral under matched contexts and steering schedules to distinguish forecast transfer from behavioral-proxy validity. Common teacher-forced prefixes and free-running generations must be interpreted separately.
7. **Improve semantic validation and replication.** The original judge covers only one of eight review templates. Plan broader blinded assessment, actual human ratings and independent feature/domain confirmation; these have not been completed.

The September 18 protocol, numerical results, PDF and raw outputs are unchanged. All new diagnostics are labeled post hoc; the new draft is not an external preregistration or a completed experiment.
