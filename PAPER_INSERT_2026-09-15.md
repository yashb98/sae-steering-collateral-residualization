## Main text

We extended the magnitude-confound analysis to downstream collateral spread in an independent replication of the four model/SAE settings, with 300 features per setting. We computed rank-based partial correlations after controlling for mean logit-effect magnitude, mean final-token feature activation and final-token firing frequency. The additive intervention coefficient was fixed at one and therefore dropped from the control set. Decoder crowding retained a positive association with raw collateral in GPT-2-small (partial rho = 0.475, 95% bootstrap CI [0.373, 0.567]) and Gemma-2-2B (0.242 [0.123, 0.362]), while the Pythia-70M estimate was near zero (-0.004 [-0.124, 0.114]). The Llama-3.1-8B estimate was smaller (0.152 [0.039, 0.266]) and did not pass Holm correction across the tested predictor family. Collateral-per-effect results followed the same broad pattern. Under these controls, the largest absolute associations were decoder crowding in GPT-2, direct-logit L2 in Pythia, encoder-decoder alignment in Gemma, and direct-logit top-10 mass in Llama; this ranking does not establish statistically significant differences between predictors. These results support setting-dependent collateral prediction beyond the measured nuisance variables.

## Appendix and limitations

The analysis uses the stated feature-frequency band and downstream panel selection in RESULTS.md. Natural activation is defined as the mean final-token activation over the context pool. Confidence intervals use 10,000 feature bootstrap resamples and condition on the dictionary and shared corpus pool. For predictive evaluation, nuisance regression and predictor scaling are fitted within each training fold. The attached `results/analysis/table_collateral_residualized.tex` reports both collateral labels and both control sets.

Three feature-sampling seeds per setting support the broad canonical pattern; they are not independently trained SAE dictionaries. Coefficient sweeps cover alpha in {0.5, 1, 2, 4} for GPT-2 and Pythia and {0.5, 1, 2} for Gemma and Llama. At alpha = 2, both Llama crowding intervals include zero. Threshold sweeps cover tau in {0.02, 0.05, 0.1, 0.2}. GPT-2, Gemma and Llama retain positive controlled estimates, though some pointwise intervals include zero. Pythia raw-count estimates become weakly positive at tau = 0.1 and 0.2; the corresponding median counts are only 6.2 and 0.9 of 2,048 panel features. These exploratory threshold comparisons are not corrected across the sweep. In particular, Pythia's collateral-per-effect interval still includes zero at tau = 0.1. Llama's canonical Holm-adjusted p-values are 0.122 for raw count and 0.063 for collateral per effect.

The context split measures reproducibility within one corpus pool; it does not establish a prediction ceiling. Matched random-direction comparisons and descriptive feature-selection summaries do not establish a causal effect of crowding or improved behavioral utility. The collateral labels count downstream SAE activation changes, without independently identifying unrelated behaviors. Independent corpus draws and independently trained dictionary seeds remain untested.
